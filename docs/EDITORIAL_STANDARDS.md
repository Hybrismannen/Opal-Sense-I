# Editorial Standards — OPAL SENSE I [OSI´]

The Opal treats dress as creative work, material construction, commercial artefact and cultural history.

## Evidence
1. Identify the author, date, publisher and context.
2. Prefer original catalogues, designers' statements, museum objects and scholarship.
3. Cross-check commercial claims with independent sources.
4. Date trend reports and prices; distinguish listings from sold transactions.
5. Record unresolved conflicts rather than silently harmonising them.
6. Each object receives an independent record. Do not compare unrelated shoes without editorial reason.
7. Keep brand status, technical design, manufacturing quality, construction, wear and price separate.
8. Respect specific regions, languages, makers and historical periods. The African continent and the diaspora are not single styles.
9. Obtain permission for imagery; an external link is not a media licence.
10. Public work excludes personally identifying or private shopping details.

## Source states
Unverified → Partially sourced → Source verified → Reviewed.

## Editorial issues
Each thematic issue must include: central question; chronology; named designers/objects; regional and social context; primary sources; independent criticism; image-rights log; reading list; outstanding research questions.

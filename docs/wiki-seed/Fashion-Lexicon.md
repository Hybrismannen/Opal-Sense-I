# Fashion Lexicon

A terminological reference to be built with documented usage and cultural precision.

## Initial terms
- **Dandyism:** a historically situated sartorial and social practice; not only formal dress.
- **Afrofuturism:** a broader creative and intellectual field; not simply a futuristic fashion aesthetic.
- **Haute couture:** distinguish its general craft meaning from institutional calendar participation.
- **Djellaba:** North African outer garment; analyse named regional and historical variants.
- **Fashion line:** a named product family or commercial line within a brand.
- **Collaboration:** distinct from acquisition, licensing or rebranding.
- **OEM / private label:** manufacturing and commercial distribution models; never assume a particular factory without evidence.
- **MSRP:** original recommended retail price; distinct from asking and sold prices.
- **Provenance:** traceable origin and history of a record or object.

Each entry is a research scaffold until independently sourced.

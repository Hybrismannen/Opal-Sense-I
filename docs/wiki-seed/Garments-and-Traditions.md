# Garments & Traditions

Reference library of individual garments, regional tailoring, construction, climate, materials, makers and living wear.

## Current work
- [The Moroccan djellaba — editorial dossier](https://github.com/Hybrismannen/Opal-Sense-I/issues/6)

## Standard object entry
Name(s) and language; place and period; maker or production context; pattern geometry; silhouette and construction; fibres and textiles; regional use; variations; contemporary interpretation; source provenance and image rights.

The maker and wearer are part of the history, not merely sources of inspiration.

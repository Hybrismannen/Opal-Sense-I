# Editorial Method

The Opal uses source-led cultural research and object-level product records.

- Date and identify each source.
- Distinguish original documentary evidence from interpretation.
- Keep house profiles and particular models separate.
- Mark unresolved model identifications and authenticity concerns.
- Never treat original retail price as current resale value.
- Credit named makers and cultural traditions precisely.
- Recheck changing trends, ownership and prices before publication.

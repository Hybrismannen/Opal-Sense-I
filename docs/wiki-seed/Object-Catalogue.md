# Object Catalogue

Each shoe, garment or accessory is a distinct entry.

## Footwear corpus
The [initial JSON catalogue](https://github.com/Hybrismannen/Opal-Sense-I/blob/main/data/shoes.json) contains 35 independently tracked discussion records. They are **not** all verified product identities.

## Standard entry fields
- Maker, design line, model, edition, year, colour and article number
- Construction, upper, midsole, outsole, details and technical properties
- Material quality, functional design, fashion positioning and cultural status **assessed separately**
- Condition and authenticity checks for the individual object
- Original retail price and second-hand observations **distinguished**
- Sources, date, rights and missing evidence

Do not auto-compare unrelated shoes.

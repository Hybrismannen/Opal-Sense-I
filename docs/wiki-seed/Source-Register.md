# Source Register

The [versioned source list](https://github.com/Hybrismannen/Opal-Sense-I/blob/main/data/sources.json) is the reference index behind editorial research.

## Evidential hierarchy
- P1: original documents, museum objects, dated collections, verified house publications
- P2: scholarship and independent specialist research
- P3: reputable dated fashion reporting and interviews
- P4: retailer claims, marketplace listings and social content
- U: unresolved or inadequately supported

A primary source does not independently verify its own marketing claims. Record the source type and cite the precise assertion.

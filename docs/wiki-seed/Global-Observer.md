# Global Observer

Fashion movements, designers, institutions, craft networks and documented trends with geographic precision.

## Regional desks
Sweden; Nordic region; continental Europe; Southern Africa; Northern Africa; East and West Africa; the African diaspora; the Americas; Asia and the Pacific. Regional groupings are research indexes, not assertions of stylistic unity.

## Editorial investigations
- [Nordic and European fashion observer](https://github.com/Hybrismannen/Opal-Sense-I/issues/8)
- [African fashion houses and couture](https://github.com/Hybrismannen/Opal-Sense-I/issues/7)

## Trend evidence
Date and scope each report. Runway collections, retail demand, street observation, resale transactions and search trends answer distinct questions and should not be presented as interchangeable.

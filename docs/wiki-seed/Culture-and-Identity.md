# Culture & Identity

The Opal explores how fashion participates in artistic, social and political identity.

## Current editorial investigations
- [Black dandyism — Special Issue 001](https://github.com/Hybrismannen/Opal-Sense-I/issues/4)
- [Fashion futures — Special Issue 002](https://github.com/Hybrismannen/Opal-Sense-I/issues/5)
- [Afropunk — Special Issue 006](https://github.com/Hybrismannen/Opal-Sense-I/issues/9)

Each investigation should name specific communities, historic settings, designers, garments, source works and editorial interpretations.

## Method
Do not reduce a cultural field to a graphic motif or generic 'look'. Build geographic and chronological evidence, prefer source voices, and distinguish modern editorial styling from historical documentation.
